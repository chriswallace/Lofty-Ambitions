# Food and shop coverage

Implement the user's requested next gameplay step: finite, nearby food and retail service so layout matters, with clear feedback identifying areas that need service. Continue the existing Swift/AppKit game and approved forgiving growth direction. Values below are implementation choices for an initial tuning pass.

Homes, offices, coworking studios, and hotel guests create one unit of food demand and one unit of shopping demand per occupant. Service venues and leisure visitors do not create recursive demand. Connected, operational restaurants supply 240 people, cafés 120, ramen bars 180, shops 320, and bookshops 140. Food reaches ±4 floors; shopping ±6. Both require room centers within 48 construction cells horizontally and lobby-connected floors. Nightly business closure does not disable neighborhood coverage; incidents and lost access do. Service capacity is independent of venue occupancy, avoiding a startup circular dependency.

Allocate finite capacity proportionally to nearby unmet demand, weighted toward closer providers. Redistribute remaining demand through residual paths, reassigning existing allocations when needed, until no useful capacity remains. Results must be deterministic, invariant to room-array order, and must not double count customers or capacity. A vacant consumer creates no current demand; its displayed availability and move-in comfort use min(1, reachable spare capacity / full room capacity), calculated independently after occupied demand and without reserving capacity. No persisted coverage counters or schema change. Arrears occupants retain demand; other active incidents suspend it.

Replace the global food/retail booleans for consumer comfort with each room's coverage: food contributes -8 to +6 happiness points, shops 0 to +4. Other amenities and rent retain existing effects. Coverage changes use existing tenant movement pacing; no immediate occupancy mutation or eviction. Existing earned levels remain permanent. Commercial/leisure rooms use neutral fully supplied comfort because their visitors are not the neighborhood demand population in this pass. Venue revenue and rush-hour journeys are deferred.

Use a single computed coverage snapshot per tenant update and per controller UI refresh. Rendering must reuse that snapshot rather than solve coverage for every room at 30 Hz. Inspectors show food and shopping percentages, distinguish unavailable versus exhausted service, and expose provider capacity/utilization. Issues groups shortages by floor, with a button to select a food/shop build tool near the affected floor. Tower info shows the same summaries. Incidents, disconnection, housekeeping, rent, and party noise retain warning priority. Catalog descriptions disclose capacity and range. Existing saves derive the new state on load.

Verify limited reach both vertically and horizontally, fair capacity sharing and conservation, multiple overlapping venues, disconnected/incident venues, basement connections, vacant rooms, order independence, saved continuation, gradual tenant response, immediate feedback after construction/removal, and native action/layout behavior. Keep macOS 13, universal packaging, and existing corridors.
